# Product Overview

## What Betsy is

Betsy is a social sports competition app built around private leagues, virtual betting, and direct rivalry between friends.

It is not a real-money sportsbook. It is a game system.

The product is designed to capture:
- prediction behavior
- group competition
- sports identity
- recurring engagement
- social tension between friends

## Problem the product solves

Sports conversations are naturally social and competitive, but the experience is fragmented.

People already:
- compare predictions
- brag about calling results
- track who "knows football" or "knows NBA"
- challenge each other informally

What is usually missing is structure.

Betsy turns that informal behavior into a persistent game:
- leagues
- rules
- scores
- history
- rankings
- direct duels

## Target user

Primary audience:
- sports fans
- friend groups
- office groups
- football and NBA communities
- users who enjoy betting-style decision making but do not want real-money risk

Secondary audience:
- creators or community admins who want structured engagement
- internal company or brand-led leagues
- groups that want a shared competition layer around sports

## Product pillars

### 1. League-based competition
The league is the backbone of the product. Users do not just open the app to place isolated picks. They enter a social context with recurring rivalry and visibility.

### 2. Strategy through constraints
The app becomes more interesting because leagues define rules:
- how many bets per day
- which competitions are allowed
- when betting is open
- what balance users start with

This creates different "game worlds" depending on the league.

### 3. Direct emotional rivalry
Arena introduces 1v1 challenges. It gives the product a sharp social edge and increases retention through personal rivalry, not just abstract ranking.

### 4. Visual and state clarity
Betsy is designed so users always understand:
- what happened
- what is open
- what is pending
- what they can do next
- how their position changed

## Current product maturity

The app should be understood as an advanced functional MVP, not just a concept mockup.

Already present:
- account flow
- Firebase-based auth
- private leagues
- join codes
- configurable league rules
- betting flow
- ticket history
- rankings
- Arena challenge system
- notifications and challenge banners
- tester/dev flows
- simulation system for unresolved real-data areas

## Long-term vision

Betsy can evolve into a larger category of social sports competition:
- real-time sports APIs
- more sports
- season systems
- richer personal stats
- premium leagues
- creator/community leagues
- sponsored competitions
- higher-quality push and retention loops

The long-term opportunity is not "fake betting".

It is building a social sports layer where prediction, competition, rivalry, and group identity all live in one product.
